Animatics

an·i·mat·ic | noun

a preliminary version of a movie made by shooting successive sections of a storyboard with a soundtrack

Since I was young, I've always loved watching animated shows ranging from Avatar: The Last Airbender to Star Wars: The Clone Wars; not just for the amazing storylines, but for the amazing art as well. As a result, I've worked to improve my own art, and have used the techniques that I've developed to tell different stories. Each of these took 30+ hours to create. Check them out below!
